3. Offline-First Architecture, Backups & Master Password Responsibility

Retail Store One operates on a local-first, offline-first architecture where operational records reside inside your device's local IndexedDB storage rather than a developer-managed central cloud database.

Critical Backup & Password Responsibility:

1. Regular Backups: You are solely responsible for maintaining regular backups of your store data—either by connecting your Google Account for automated encrypted Google Drive appDataFolder synchronization or by exporting encrypted local .json.gz backup files.

Because we operate a zero-knowledge vault with no backdoor or server-side password storage, if you lose or forget your Master Password and cannot answer your registered recovery questions, your encrypted cloud backups cannot be decrypted or recovered by anyone.
