Core Engineering

1. Offline-First Local Storage & Multi-Terminal Architecture

Retail Store One (formerly KiranaPro) eliminates recurring SaaS subscriptions, slow cloud-dependent billing screens, and central server data harvesting. Every billing counter executes locally on the merchant's Android tablet, phone, or browser with zero network latency—continuing uninterrupted through internet outages, weak cellular signals, or power fluctuations.

Client-Side IndexedDB Engine KiranaDB

All product catalogs, sales invoices, customer credit ledgers, supplier purchase orders, expense vouchers, stock movements, and cash drawer sessions are stored locally in structured IndexedDB object stores (idb).

  • Zero External Server Dependency: No remote server.url or proprietary cloud database is required to boot, bill, scan barcodes, or print receipts.
  • Multi-Store Support: Manage multiple independent retail branches or business entities on the same device, each isolated in its own dedicated database (kiranapro_<storeId>).
  • Instant Live Tab Sync: Uses BroadcastChannel('kiranapro_local_sync') to synchronize multiple open browser windows locally in real time.

Conflict-Free Multi-Device Merging HLC + Commutative

Designed for shops running a main counter tablet alongside secondary staff smartphones offline simultaneously, reconciling changes deterministically when internet connectivity returns.

  • Device-Prefixed Invoice Numbers: Each terminal receives an assigned device code (A, B, C...) generating collision-free invoice sequences (e.g., INV-A-2627-001 vs. INV-B-2627-001).
  • Commutative Stock Ledger: Inventory levels are computed from append-only additive StockMovement deltas so simultaneous offline sales merge cleanly with automatic negative-stock alerts.
  • Hybrid Logical Clocks (HLC): Causality-ordered HLC timestamps resolve concurrent catalog edits (product titles, prices, categories) deterministically.
  • Idempotent Event Replay: Every sync packet carries a unique event ID; replayed or duplicate delta logs are automatically skipped so customer payments are never double-counted.

Staged Restoration & Data Portability Atomic Rollback

Built-in safeguards protect active shop databases against interrupted network transfers, corrupt files, or storage quota errors.

  • Atomic Staged Restore: Cloud or file backups are first decrypted, checksum-verified, and validated inside a temporary staging database (kiranapro_staging_<storeId>) before atomically committing to live storage.
  • Zero-Loss Rollback: If decryption fails or a storage error occurs mid-restore, the staging database is discarded and the live shop database remains 100% untouched.
  • Portable Offline Archives: Export and import compressed, password-protected .json.gz backup bundles directly to device storage anytime.
Front-Counter & Stock Operations

2. High-Speed POS Billing, Barcode Scanning & Inventory Control

Engineered for fast-moving Indian retail counters—supporting packaged goods, loose weight/volume items, multi-cart billing, packet counting, and batch/expiry inventory tracking.

Smart POS Checkout & Cart Management

  • Instant Product Lookup: Search items in milliseconds by name, regional alias, SKU, category, or barcode—or tap directly from the visual Quick-Items grid.
  • Built-In Camera Barcode & QR Scanner: Integrated ScannerHubModal and BarcodeScannerModal (powered by html5-qrcode) scan product barcodes directly using the phone or tablet camera.
  • Loose Decimal Quantities & Packet Counter: Dedicated DecimalQuantityModal and PacketCountModal handle loose weights and volumes (kg, gm, ltr, ml, mtr) as well as multi-pack bundles.
  • Coupons, Item & Bill Discounts: Apply percentage or flat rupee discounts on individual line items or entire bills via CouponManagerModal.
  • Hold & Recall Multiple Bills: Park pending customer carts (HeldBillsModal) while attending to another customer and resume with one tap.
  • Multi-Tender & Split Payments: Accept Cash, UPI (with dynamic store UPI QR generation via UpiQrPaymentModal), Card, or Udhaar Credit—including split payments across multiple modes (SplitPaymentModal).

Comprehensive Inventory & Stock Movement Engine

  • Detailed SKU Master: Track product name, brand, category, unit of measure, MRP, selling price, purchase/cost price, HSN/SAC code, GST tax slab (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%), barcode, and product image.
  • Batch & Expiry Management: Record batch numbers and expiry dates for groceries, FMCG, dairy, agricultural inputs, and pharmacy items to prevent expired stock sales.
  • Real-Time Stock Adjustments: Perform rapid stock-in, damage/wastage write-offs, or physical audit corrections via QuickStockChangeModal.
  • Low-Stock & Reorder Watchlist: Automatic low-stock alerts highlight items below minimum threshold with one-click WhatsApp/Arattai supplier reorder messaging.
  • Bulk CSV Catalog Import & Export: Import hundreds of products from CSV spreadsheets (csvParser.ts) or export full stock valuation sheets.
Accounting, Khata & Compliance

3. Customer Udhaar Khata, Supplier Purchase Orders, Cash Closing & GST Reports

Complete back-office financial accounting built into the same offline app—eliminating separate khata notebooks, expense registers, and manual GST calculators.

Double-Entry Customer Udhaar Khata

  • Customer Directory & Credit Limits: Store customer names, phone numbers, addresses, profile photos, GSTINs, and maximum credit limits.
  • Derived Running Balances: Every credit sale and payment receipt (ReceiveUdhaarPaymentModal) posts to a double-entry ledger with automatic balance computation.
  • Digital Khata Statements & Reminders: Export itemized customer ledger statements (udharStatementExport.ts) as PDF/image and share payment reminders via WhatsApp, SMS, or Gmail.
  • Payment Due Scheduling: Track overdue balances and schedule follow-up collection reminders (ScheduleCalendarReminderModal).

Suppliers, Purchase Orders & Cash Register

  • Supplier Directory & Payables: Track wholesale distributors, supplier phone/GST details, purchase bills, and outstanding supplier dues.
  • Purchase Order to Invoice Conversion: Create supplier purchase orders (SupplierOrderModal) and convert received deliveries directly into stock-inward purchase invoices (ConvertPoToInvoiceModal).
  • Business Expense Tracker: Categorize and record daily shop expenses (rent, electricity, transport, packaging, staff wages) in ExpensesScreen.
  • Daily Cash Drawer Closing: Reconcile opening cash, cash sales, cash payouts, and physical drawer count at end-of-day in CashClosingScreen.

GST Invoicing & Business Analytics

  • GST-Compliant Tax Breakdown: Automatic intra-state (CGST + SGST) and inter-state (IGST) tax computation per HSN code and tax slab.
  • GSTR-1 Ready Reports: Generate B2B and B2C sales summaries, tax slab breakdowns, and downloadable CSV/PDF reports for tax filing.
  • Interactive Business Dashboards: Analyze revenue trends, gross and net profit margins, top-selling products, payment mode mix, and custom date ranges (DashboardScreen & ReportsScreen).
  • Customizable In-App Widgets: Pin live Quick-Glance sales tickers, Cash Drawer status, Urgent Reorder alerts, Udhaar Watchlist, Profit Meter, and Daily Sales Goal trackers right on your dashboard (StoreWidgetsGrid & FloatingStoreWidget).

Native Android Integration

6. Native Android Thermal Printers, Home Screen Widgets & OS Bridges

The native Android APK (in.kiranapro.pos) embeds custom Java Capacitor plugins inside MainActivity.java to communicate directly with retail counter peripherals and Android OS services without third-party bridge apps.

ESC/POS Thermal Printer Plugin ThermalPrinterPlugin

Direct binary ESC/POS printing for 58mm and 80mm thermal receipt rolls across three hardware transports:

  • Bluetooth SPP Thermal Printers: Native RFCOMM socket pairing, printer auto-detection (TVS, Epson, Rugtek, Everycom, Hoin, NGX, Retsol), and chunked ESC/POS transmission with Android 12+ runtime permissions (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN).
  • USB OTG POS Printers: Android USB Host bulk-out endpoint discovery and direct OTG cable receipt printing.
  • Wi-Fi / LAN Network Printers: Direct TCP port 9100 socket printing and fast local subnet printer discovery.
  • Android System PrintManager: Native A4/A5 PDF and HTML document printing via AndroidNativeBridge.printHtmlContent().

Android Home Screen Widgets StoreWidgetPlugin

Native Android AppWidgetProvider widgets keep store performance visible and launch billing in one tap:

  • Full Store Dashboard Widget (4×2 Resizable): Powered by StoreSummaryWidgetProvider—displays live daily revenue, bill count, and key store metrics directly on your phone or tablet home screen.
  • Quick-POS Launcher Widget (2×2 Resizable): Powered by QuickPosWidgetProvider—compact counter widget with deep-link intents that open MainActivity directly into a new POS billing screen.

Native Share, Email & Tablet Insets AndroidNativeBridge

Purpose-built Android system bridges ensure smooth operation on both compact phones and large retail tablets:

  • Native Share & Direct Gmail Dispatch: Shares generated PDF invoices, JPG receipts, and GSTR CSV reports via Android's native chooser or pre-addressed Gmail intents using secure FileProvider URIs.
  • Edge-to-Edge Tablet Safe Insets: Dynamic handling of status bars, navigation bars, display cutouts, and soft keyboards (WindowInsetsCompat) prevents UI clipping on Samsung Galaxy Tabs and Android phones.

Cryptographic Vault & Access Control

8. Zero-Knowledge Encryption & Role-Based Store Security

Built with defense-in-depth cryptography to protect store owners against unauthorized staff edits, device theft, and cloud data exposure.

Zero-Knowledge AES-256-GCM Cloud Vault

  • PBKDF2-SHA256 Key Derivation: Encryption keys are derived locally from the Store Owner's Master Password using PBKDF2-SHA256 with 200,000 iterations and a cryptographically random 16-byte salt.
  • AES-256-GCM Authenticated Encryption: Backups use 256-bit AES-GCM with a unique 96-bit random IV, 128-bit authentication tag, Authenticated Additional Data (AAD binding storeId and ownerGoogleSub), and pre-decryption SHA-256 checksum verification.
  • In-Memory Non-Extractable Keys: Derived CryptoKey instances remain strictly in volatile session memory and are never written to localStorage or disk.

Counter Staff Permissions & PIN Safeguards

  • Owner vs. Staff Separation: Store Owners retain full administrative authority while Billing Staff accounts can be restricted to front-counter billing and customer lookup.
  • Sensitive Action PIN Re-Authentication: Modifying store GSTIN, updating UPI payment ID, changing staff permissions, importing external backups, or resetting the store database requires explicit 4–6 digit PIN verification (verifySecurityCredential).
  • 3-Question Owner Recovery & Audit Trail: Local password recovery via PBKDF2-hashed security questions (InitialOwnerSetupModal) paired with a tamper-evident activity log (AuditTrailTab).
